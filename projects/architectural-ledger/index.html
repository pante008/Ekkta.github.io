Project Summary & Objective

Problem statement

Operations teams were switching across 3–5 tools per incident, causing context loss, delay, and low trust in AI alerts. The objective was a single explainable UX flow that supports faster and more confident decisions.

User Research & Insights

  • 5 moderated interviews across operations, finance, and engineering roles
  • Recurring pain: slow validation and poor handoff continuity
  • Trust gap: users wanted confidence scores plus evidence visibility

Design Approach & Usability Testing

  • Research synthesis → task flow mapping → low-fi wireframing
  • 23 high-fidelity Figma screens across 5 key tasks
  • 5 moderated usability sessions (think-aloud), transcript + timing capture

Solution Overview / Demo

Final solution includes alert detection, guided root-cause analysis, ticket creation, report generation with review, and one-tap sharing to collaboration tools.

Usability Insights (Feedback Snippets + Impact)

“We jumped from step 2 to step 5.”

Impact: enforced step-by-step progression with clear step counter.

“I couldn’t confirm sharing finished.”

Impact: added explicit post-share success confirmation screen.

“Preview made me trust what gets sent.”

Impact: split report flow into Generate → Preview → Send.

AI Tools Used Throughout

  • Looppanel: interview synthesis and thematic clustering
  • Stitch AI: low-fi/hi-fi iteration support
  • Claude + Figma MCP: heuristic checks and cross-screen consistency
  • Otter.ai: transcript extraction for evidence-backed redesigns
  • ChatGPT/Julius: scoring logic and visual analytics support

Key Takeaways & Design Improvements

  • Explainability drives AI trust more than speed alone
  • Mandatory confirmation states prevent workflow ambiguity
  • Mixed-method evaluation (qual + quant) catches critical blind spots
  • Next iteration: confidence formula transparency and richer evidence traces
